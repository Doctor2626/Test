How to use this manual

Chapters run in build order. Each one opens with what must be finished before the trade starts and ends with the condition the space has to be in when the trade hands off. Methods tables compare the ways to do the work; the ESC default line under each says which we normally choose. Amber callouts are inspection holds: no covering work until they pass. Codes cited are model codes (IRC 2021, NEC 2023, TCNA). Local amendments and the building department govern.
