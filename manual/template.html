How to use this manual

The manual runs in build order, in five parts. Each chapter opens with what must be finished before the trade starts and ends with the condition the space has to be in when the trade hands off. Methods tables compare the ways to do the work; the green ESC default line under each says which we normally choose. Gold callouts are inspection holds: nothing gets covered until they pass. Appendix A lists every hold in the book and Appendix B every ESC default, for quick reference on site.

Codes cited are model codes (IRC 2021, NEC 2023, TCNA). Local amendments and the building department govern.
